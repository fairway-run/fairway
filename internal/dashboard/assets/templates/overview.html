What changes

The work remains understandable after the conversation ends.

01

Context survives

The task, decisions, checkpoints, and handbacks remain durable when a provider stops or a context window resets.

02

Claims cite facts

Completion and readiness point to commands, artifacts, reviews, commits, and externally owned execution records.

03

Authority stays explicit

Fairway can expose readiness and gaps without silently acquiring approval, merge, deploy, release, or risk authority.

How teams operate

Collaborate on uncertainty. Delegate only bounded work.

Collaborate while intent, constraints, ownership, or proof remain unsettled. Bound one independently checkable result before delegation, then verify and challenge it with evidence. If the evidence changes the diagnosis, return to collaboration before promotion.

Then choose the smallest execution surface that fits: every material attachment points back to a Fairway task, and provider surfaces can end without losing intent, evidence, judgment, or handback state.

Short and bounded

Subagent

Use within the current task when the work does not need a separate human conversation.

Return findings or evidence, reconcile the task, then end the attachment.
Independent interaction

Task-specific thread

Use for review, approvals, long waits, or continuity across provider turns.

Record the handback, end the session, and archive the thread after closeout.
Recurring accountability

Durable control surface

Use for a small number of cross-task decision and steering functions.

Keep it narrow and current; its transcript never replaces the Fairway record.

Durable: task, decisions, evidence, reviews, checkpoints, and handbacks. Replaceable: provider, thread, subagent, shell, and model.

Review independence is routed, not inferred: a separate thread does not replace a distinct reviewer identity or the configured review domain. Material delegated work still requires a registered session.
